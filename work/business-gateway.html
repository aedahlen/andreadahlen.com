Business Gateway

Applying agile and lean methods as lead product designer on the bgateway.com redevelopment — a site that had outgrown its own structure.

Client
Business Gateway
Agency
Whitespace
Role
Lead Product Designer
Rating
WAI ‘A’

Aim

Grow understanding, credibility and relevance among growth businesses without compromising the service delivered to start-ups — improving the experience of the site, and providing solutions for users who are time-poor.

Background

In the four years since the previous site launched, user and site requirements had shifted. New functionality and content had been bolted onto individual areas rather than addressed holistically. Each addition was reasonable on its own; none of them added up to a structure.

It was time for a proper redevelopment rather than another extension.

Key tasks

  • Applying lean methodologies
  • Research and running workshops
  • Ideation, wireframing, user-centred design
  • Adhering to accessibility standards
  • Developing a pattern library
  • Collaboration with web developers and creative designers
  • Usability testing

Research

A detailed UX review delivered insights and recommendations, alongside research conducted with Progressive and Business Gateway to identify user needs.

Content improvements

  • Removing gated content
  • Surfacing popular guides
  • A more dynamic, less hierarchical structure
  • More actionable “how to” content
  • Filters instead of hierarchical navigation
  • Clearer separation of audiences by business stage

Audience needs

  • Growth businesses wanted a personal, tailored service, and saw Business Gateway as a generalist rather than a specialist
  • Start-ups sought help, advice, networking and best practice
  • Both groups were time-poor, and wanted content delivered to them rather than hunted for

Information architecture

The new structure made it easier to find content depending on whether you were starting or running a business. Case studies and inspiration stories were grouped under a new Success Stories gateway, a new Your Sector section served industry-specific content, and Local Office pages were folded into the main site instead of sitting as separate microsites.

Lean methodology

Heaps of wireframes came before any build — it is cheaper to scrap paper than code. Rather than waterfall, the project ran on agile with regular co-creation workshops, so client feedback landed while there was still room to be iterative, instead of arriving at one final review.

Work was broken into two UX sprints, two design sprints and four development sprints. I was involved throughout every one — not only designing functionality, but acting as custodian of the product's behaviour and design from vision through to finished product.

The tailored tool

A personalisation tool serves content based on business stage, geography and industry sector. A food and drink start-up in Dundee sees resources for starting a business, local events, market reports and the Business Gateway services relevant to them.

The design brief I set myself was to keep it simple, short, optional and in plain language — three questions a time-poor owner can answer immediately, rather than a form.

Results

85%Of users completing the tool's three steps clicked through to a recommended resource
+392%Path-to-market report downloads
+20.5%More goals completed on-site
4.05Pages per session, up from 3.81

Mobile pages per session rose 23%, and the site reached a WAI ‘A’ accessibility rating.

Phase 2 & beyond

With the MVP live, Phase 2 added online resource search, a mega nav, an expanded Your Sector section, a new case study header, events and workshops, and DigitalBoost — a Digital Scotland-funded programme helping businesses build digital skills, which went live in April 2019.

With limited time and budget for user testing during the build, I leaned heavily on close co-creation with the client and on prior research, to avoid designing in a silo — while continuing to push for formal testing and further accessibility work in later phases, including reducing PDFs on the site and working toward full AA compliance.
