Famly — a speculative concept, built rather than described

From an early concept to a clickable prototype, built with Claude Code.

Type
Speculative
Role
Product Designer
Tools
Claude, Claude Code
Year
2026

How I work now

AI has changed what I consider my job as a designer to be. Tools like Claude and Cursor let me explore, prototype and ship much faster, but that makes design judgement more important, not less: understanding the problem and the human on the other side, and bringing direction, accessibility, craft and taste to what we build.

Recently, I used Claude Code to take an early concept from idea to clickable prototype. That combination of human judgement and new ways of making is exactly what excites me about Famly's approach to AI, experimentation and learning.

The prototype

A live, clickable prototype — built end-to-end through conversation rather than starting in Figma.

aedahlen.github.io/famly Open live prototype ↗

It is deployed and public, so the most useful thing I can show is the thing itself rather than a description of it.
