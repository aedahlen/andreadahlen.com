I grew up between Finland, California and Denmark, spent ten years in the UK, and I'm settled in Copenhagen now. Moving around made me quick to spot a service that assumes everyone has the same language, address format or way of asking for help.

I care about accessibility because it decides who gets to use something at all. I care about behavioural design because what people do and what they say they'll do are rarely the same thing.

Off screen: our Finnish summerhouse, blueberries, mushrooms and my daughter.

Accessibility, built in from the start

At Shelter I wrote accessibility into the design system and the brand guidelines, so new pages met WCAG 2.1 AA by default. The guides are still published.

Behavioural design

My Goldsmiths dissertation tested nudges against clinical waste in an NHS trust. I still start from what people actually do, then make the right path the easy one.

Research that ends in a decision

I led a team of eight at Shelter Scotland. With clients I boil research down to a short, ranked list, so the team knows what to do on Monday.
