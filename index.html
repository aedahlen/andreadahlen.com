About

A Swedish-speaking Finn — think Moomin

I was raised between California, Finland and Denmark, and I'm now Copenhagen-based. I'm a freelance UX/UI designer with over a decade of experience in digital, working mostly in regulated, high-complexity environments — healthcare, public sector and enterprise — where governance and inclusion matter.

I approach design methodically: user flows and requirements before visual polish, research that turns into action, and close collaboration with product, engineering and subject-matter teams. Outside of work you'll find me writing, running, going to gigs, and spending time with my daughter.
