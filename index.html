I grew up between California, Finland and Denmark, spent ten years in the UK, and I'm now firmly back in Copenhagen. Moving around made me quick to notice when a service assumes everyone speaks the same language or asks for help the same way.

Most of my work has been on services people turn to when things go wrong: housing advice, cervical screening, support for small businesses. It taught me two things. Accessibility decides who gets to use something at all. And people do what's easy far more often than what they plan to.

Off screen, I'm happiest at our Finnish summerhouse, foraging for mushrooms and blueberries with my daughter.
