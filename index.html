About

A Swedish-speaking Finn: think Moomin

Happiest at my Finnish summerhouse, foraging for blueberries and mushrooms, and spending time with my daughter.

Raised between Finland, California and Denmark and a decade spent in the UK, I have firmly planted my roots back in Copenhagen. It's the reason I notice when a service assumes everyone shares one language, one address format, one way of asking for help.

Most of my work has been where getting it wrong has consequences: housing advice for people in crisis, cervical screening for the people the NHS wasn't reaching, business support for owners with no time to spare. In that world accessibility isn't a finishing step. It decides who can use the thing at all.

I start from what people do rather than what they say they'll do, a habit picked up writing a dissertation on behavioural design, and I'd rather settle flows and requirements before anyone argues about visual polish. Still curious about the humans on both ends of the screen.
